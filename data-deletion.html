Data deletion — NajotSoft CRM

Updated: 11 November 2026

For business users

  1. Disconnect Instagram: in the panel, open Sozlamalar → Kanallar (Settings → Channels) and press Uzish (Disconnect) next to the Instagram account. New messages and comments stop reaching us.
  2. Delete all data: email quroqovshoxrux@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", giving your company name and the email you registered with. Within 30 days we delete your account and all its data (conversations, contacts, files, platform tokens) and confirm by email.

For people who messaged a business on Instagram

Email quroqovshoxrux@gmail.com with your Instagram username and the business you messaged. Within 30 days we delete your messages and profile data and confirm by email.

Remove the app's access on Instagram

In the Instagram app: Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, select NajotSoft CRM and press Remove.
