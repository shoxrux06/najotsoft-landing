2. Xizmat nima qiladi

NajotSoft CRM bizneslarga mijozlari bilan yozishmalarni (Telegram, Instagram, WhatsApp, SMS) va qo'ng'iroqlarni bitta panelda yuritish hamda mijozlar bazasini (CRM) boshqarish imkonini beradi. Ma'lumotlardan reklama uchun foydalanmaymiz va ularni sotmaymiz.

Privacy Policy — NajotSoft CRM

Effective date: 11 November 2026

1. Who we are

NajotSoft CRM is provided by QUROQOV SHOXRUX DAGAR O'G'LI (NajotSoft, Tashkent, Uzbekistan). Contact: quroqovshoxrux@gmail.com.

2. What the service does

NajotSoft CRM lets businesses handle conversations with their customers (Telegram, Instagram, WhatsApp, SMS) and phone calls in one shared inbox, and manage customer records (CRM). We process data on behalf of the businesses that use the service, and only so that they can use it.

3. Data we collect

  • Business users: name, email, password (stored as a hash), company name.
  • Accounts a business connects: for example the Instagram professional account ID and username, and the access token the platform issues (stored encrypted).
  • People who message a business: the name, username and profile details the platform provides; message text and attachments (images, video, audio, files); comments on the business's posts; a phone number the person shares.
  • Calls (if the business enables telephony): call details, the recording and its transcript.

4. How we use data

  • To show messages to the business's staff and deliver their replies.
  • To keep customer records, leads, tasks and orders.
  • If the business enables them: AI reply suggestions and call analysis.
  • To keep the service secure and fix errors.

We do not use data for advertising and we do not sell it.

5. Who we share data with

Only where the service needs it:

  • messaging platforms (Meta, Telegram and others), to deliver replies;
  • hosting and storage providers;
  • AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google): message text is sent only when the business enables the AI assistant, to draft a reply suggestion;
  • payment providers (Payme, Click), only for the business's subscription payments;
  • competent authorities where the law requires it.

6. Where and how long we keep data

Data is stored on servers in the Republic of Uzbekistan. It is kept while the business account is active. When a business closes its account or asks us to delete its data, the data is deleted within 30 days.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Platform access tokens are stored encrypted. Each business can see only its own data.

8. Your rights

To ask about your data, correct it or have it deleted, write to quroqovshoxrux@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. How deletion works: Data deletion. Data is processed under the Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan "On Personal Data".

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date.
